Delete your Math Infinitum account

This deletes the online account that Math Infinitum’s ranked Daily, challenges and AI tutor use, at once and for good: your name, your ranked and challenge results, the challenges you made, your rating, league and season standings, your deletion code, the reports you made, and the AI tutor’s counts and the replies you reported.

If you still have the app, it is quicker there: Settings → Online account → Delete online account.

You need the deletion code that Settings shows under Online account, and your name exactly as it appears on the board (capital letters do not matter). An account with no name — one made only for the AI tutor — needs the code alone: leave the name empty. Because the account is anonymous, the code is the only proof that it is yours. Without it, the account cannot be deleted on request.

Leave it empty if your account has no name.

Lessons and practice are stored only on the phone and are not affected. Other players’ ratings keep the points they won or lost against you, with nothing that leads back to you. The server’s technical logs of requests are kept only for the short period Supabase’s service plan sets, and then expire.
