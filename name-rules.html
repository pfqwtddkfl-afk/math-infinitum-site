Name rules

Updated 19 September 2026

The name you choose for the ranked Daily and challenges is shown to other players beside your results: on the daily board to everybody, and in a challenge to whoever opens it. Choosing one means you accept these rules.

A name must not

  • insult, threaten or harass anybody, or attack people for who they are;
  • be sexual, violent or about drugs or crime;
  • pretend to be another person, a moderator, the developer or Math Infinitum;
  • contain personal information — yours or anybody’s full name, phone number, email address, home address or social media handle;
  • advertise anything or point to a website.

How names are checked

A name is checked automatically when it is saved, and some are refused however they are spelled. Any player can report a name from the daily board, and reports are reviewed.

A name that breaks these rules is removed: the player keeps their results and rating and is asked for a new name before their next ranked day. An account that keeps breaking them may be deleted.

Your own screen

On the daily board you can hide any player from your phone. Settings lists the players you have hidden and lets you show them again.
