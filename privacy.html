Privacy policy

Updated 19 September 2026

Math Infinitum keeps your learning on your phone: nothing you do in lessons, reviews or practice is sent anywhere unless you ask the AI tutor something. Only if you join the ranked Daily, make or take a challenge, or turn on the AI tutor does the app make an online account — an anonymous one, holding the name you choose, if you choose one, and your results — and you can delete it whenever you like.

Who is responsible

Math Infinitum is a mathematics learning app. “We” in this policy means its developer, who decides how the data described here is used.

To ask about your data or anything in this policy, write to mathpath6316@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do while learning is recorded only on your phone, in the app’s private storage: lessons you have finished and the ones in progress, your answers, your review schedule, Survival and practice runs, practice ratings, badges, and players you have hidden. None of it is sent anywhere, except what you choose to send the AI tutor when you ask it something (below).

If you have an online account (below), the phone also keeps its session and its deletion code, and whether you agreed to the tutor. The tutor's conversation is kept only while the app is open, and never written to the phone's storage.

Settings → Erase progress on this phone removes the learning record. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. If Android backup is switched on for your phone, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup, which Google stores and protects under your Google account; we never receive it.

What we keep, and only if you join the ranked Daily or a challenge

Joining the ranked Daily is optional, and so is making or taking a challenge: both use the same online account. When you join, the app makes an anonymous one: no email, phone number or password is asked for. Our service then holds:

  • a random account identifier, created by the sign-in service;
  • the name you choose;
  • for each ranked day you play: when you started and finished, how many answers were right and your score. Your answers are marked and not kept;
  • your rating, your league and your standing in the weekly season, worked out from those results;
  • for each challenge you make: its code, its fifteen questions and when it expires;
  • for each challenge you play: when you started and finished, how many answers were right and how long you took — and in a duel, which of the fifteen you got right and how long each took. Your answers are marked and not kept;
  • a one-way hash of your deletion code — never the code itself;
  • any report you make about another player’s name: whose name, the reason you picked and when.

The AI tutor, only if you turn it on

The AI tutor is optional, and lessons never need it. It asks before its first reply. When you ask it for a hint, an explanation or a check of your working, the app sends which lesson and question you are on, your answer to it, and any working you type — nothing else about you. It uses the same anonymous online account as the ranked Daily, made when you turn the tutor on if this phone has none. Our service then holds:

  • the account's random identifier and a one-way hash of its deletion code, as for the ranked Daily;
  • for each day you use the tutor, how many replies you asked for and how long they were (in tokens), to share it fairly — never what you asked or typed;
  • replies to questions that do not depend on who asked (no typed working), kept with no account attached, so the next reader gets the same reply at once;
  • a reply you report: the lesson and question it was about, the reply and the reason you picked. Your working is not kept, though a reply that checks your working may quote a line of it.

What other players can see

Your name, your results on the daily board, your rating and your league standing are shown to other players in the app. That is what a ranking is. A challenge shows the name of whoever made it, and the names and results of everybody who has played it, to anybody who opens it with its code; in a duel, the other player also sees which of the fifteen you got right and how long each took. Nothing else about you is shown, and nobody can see who reported a name.

Who processes the data

Everything our service holds, listed above, is stored by Supabase, which provides the database, sign-in and server functions, in the European Union (Ireland), and processes the data on our behalf. Home and the Arena screen also read the current ranked season and event from it, whether or not you have joined. Like any server, Supabase records technical logs of these requests — including IP address, device information and time — and keeps them for a short period set by its service plan.

What you send the AI tutor is also processed, on our behalf, by Groq, Inc., a company in the United States that runs the AI model in its data centres in the United States and other countries. Groq is sent the lesson's own text, your answer and your working; not your account identifier. It does not use them to train models, and with its zero data retention, which we have switched on, it does not log or store them.

The Videos tab shows videos from YouTube. When the tab opens, the picture of each video is loaded from YouTube (i.ytimg.com), and a video you open plays in YouTube’s embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com), which loads what it needs from Google’s servers. These requests give YouTube and Google your IP address and device information, under Google’s privacy policy. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you open the Videos tab.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anybody for their own purposes.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

How long it is kept

  • The online account and everything listed above: until you delete the account.
  • An anonymous account that never chose a name, never played a ranked day and has not used the AI tutor in the last 30 days is deleted automatically once it is seven days old.
  • A challenge and everybody's results in it: until a week after it expires, and a challenge expires two weeks after it is made.
  • The AI tutor's daily counts and the replies you report: 90 days. A shared reply with no account attached: 30 days.
  • What you send the AI tutor: not logged or stored by Groq (zero data retention).
  • A report: until the account that made it or the account it names is deleted.
  • Data on your phone: until you erase it in Settings or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Online account → Delete online account. The account is deleted at once, together with your name, your ranked and challenge results, the challenges you made, your rating, league and season standings, your deletion code, the reports you made, and the AI tutor's counts and the replies you reported.

Without the app: on the Delete your account page of the Math Infinitum website, with the deletion code that Settings shows, and your name if the account has one. Because the account is anonymous, the code is the only proof that a request comes from you; without it we cannot tell your account from somebody else’s, so we cannot delete it on request.

An account with no name — one made only for the AI tutor — is deleted on that page by its deletion code alone. It is also deleted automatically once it is seven days old and the tutor has not been used for 30 days.

After deletion, other players’ ratings keep the points they won or lost against you, with nothing that leads back to you, and the server’s technical logs expire on their own schedule.

Children

The ranked Daily, with its public names and boards, and the AI tutor are not intended for children under 13. If a child under 13 has joined the ranked Daily or turned on the tutor, delete the online account in Settings.

Security

Everything the app sends travels encrypted (HTTPS), and so does what our server sends Groq. The database lets each player write only their own name and reports, lets nobody read another player’s deletion code, and lets no player write a score: the server marks the ranked Daily and every challenge itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a change to what is collected will be described in the app before it applies.
