Privacy policy

Updated 16 September 2026

Math Infinitum keeps your learning on your phone: nothing you do in lessons, reviews or practice is sent anywhere. Only if you join the ranked Daily does the app make an online account — an anonymous one, holding the name you choose and your ranked results — and you can delete it whenever you like.

Who is responsible

Math Infinitum is a mathematics learning app. “We” in this policy means its developer, who decides how the data described here is used.

To ask about your data or anything in this policy, write to mathpath6316@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do while learning is recorded only on your phone, in the app’s private storage: lessons you have finished and the ones in progress, your answers, your review schedule, Survival and practice runs, practice ratings, badges, and players you have hidden. None of it is sent anywhere.

If you join the ranked Daily, the phone also keeps the session of your online account and its deletion code.

Settings → Erase progress on this phone removes the learning record. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. If Android backup is switched on for your phone, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup, which Google stores and protects under your Google account; we never receive it.

What we keep, and only if you join the ranked Daily

Joining the ranked Daily is optional. When you join, the app makes an anonymous online account: no email, phone number or password is asked for. Our service then holds:

  • a random account identifier, created by the sign-in service;
  • the name you choose;
  • for each ranked day you play: when you started and finished, how many answers were right and your score. Your answers are marked and not kept;
  • your rating, your league and your standing in the weekly season, worked out from those results;
  • a one-way hash of your deletion code — never the code itself;
  • any report you make about another player’s name: whose name, the reason you picked and when.

What other players can see

Your name, your results on the daily board, your rating and your league standing are shown to other players in the app. That is what a ranking is. Nothing else about you is shown, and nobody can see who reported a name.

Who processes the data

The online account, the ranked results and the reports are stored by Supabase, which provides the database, sign-in and server functions, in the European Union (Ireland), and processes the data on our behalf. The Arena screen also reads the current ranked season from it, whether or not you have joined. Like any server, Supabase records technical logs of these requests — including IP address, device information and time — and keeps them for a short period set by its service plan.

The Videos tab shows videos from YouTube. When the tab opens, the picture of each video is loaded from YouTube (i.ytimg.com), and a video you open plays in YouTube’s embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com), which loads what it needs from Google’s servers. These requests give YouTube and Google your IP address and device information, under Google’s privacy policy. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you open the Videos tab.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anybody for their own purposes.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

How long it is kept

  • The online account and everything listed above: until you delete the account.
  • An anonymous account that never chose a name and never played a ranked day is deleted automatically seven days after it was made.
  • A report: until the account that made it or the account it names is deleted.
  • Data on your phone: until you erase it in Settings or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Online account → Delete online account. The account is deleted at once, together with your name, results, rating, league and season standings, your deletion code and the reports you made.

Without the app: on the Delete your account page of the Math Infinitum website, with your name and your deletion code, which Settings shows. Because the account is anonymous, the code is the only proof that a request comes from you; without it we cannot tell your account from somebody else’s, so we cannot delete it on request.

After deletion, other players’ ratings keep the points they won or lost against you, with nothing that leads back to you, and the server’s technical logs expire on their own schedule.

Children

The ranked Daily, with its public names and boards, is not intended for children under 13. If a child under 13 has joined it, delete the online account in Settings.

Security

Everything the app sends travels encrypted (HTTPS). The database lets each player write only their own name and reports, lets nobody read another player’s deletion code, and lets no player write a score: the server marks the ranked Daily itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a change to what is collected will be described in the app before it applies.
